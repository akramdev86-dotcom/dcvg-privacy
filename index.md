# Privacy Policy – DCVG Data Logger

**Last Updated: November 7, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

DCVG Data Logger is a professional mobile application designed to assist engineers and field technicians in conducting Direct Current Voltage Gradient (DCVG) surveys and pipeline coating inspections.

This Privacy Policy explains how the application accesses, processes, stores, and handles information when users interact with its features.

By using DCVG Data Logger, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information Accessed by the Application

### 2.1 Location Information

DCVG Data Logger requests access to the device's location through Android location services.

The application uses:
- Precise location (GPS).
- Approximate location, when available.

Location information is used to:
- Record geographical coordinates of survey campaigns.
- Associate GPS coordinates with pipeline inspection points.
- Document the geographical position of coating defects.
- Generate location-based survey reports.

Location information is stored as part of the user's local survey records.

The application does not require continuous background location tracking for its standard survey recording features.

### 2.2 Survey and Technical Information

Users may manually enter and store technical information, including:
- Survey campaign names and dates.
- Inspection point information.
- GPS coordinates.
- DCVG measurement values.
- Defect classifications.
- Signal positions and other technical observations.
- Generated survey records and reports.

This information is used exclusively to support survey documentation, data management, and reporting features.

## 3. Data Storage and Processing

DCVG Data Logger is designed to store survey information locally on the user's Android device.

The application does not require users to create an account or register with the developer.

The application does not operate a developer-managed account system or survey database server based on its current configuration.

Android operating system backup features may process eligible application data according to the device configuration, Android backup settings, and the user's Google account settings.

## 4. File Exporting and Sharing

The application provides functionality for generating PDF and CSV reports.

Users may choose to save, export, or share these files through Android's system file management and sharing mechanisms.

When users voluntarily share exported files with other applications, recipients or third-party services may process the information contained in those files according to their own privacy policies.

The developer does not control information after users independently share or transfer exported files.

## 5. Third-Party Services

DCVG Data Logger uses Android and Google Play Services components, including Google Play Services Location, to support location-related functionality.

These components may process technical or location-related information as necessary to provide their services, subject to the applicable Google privacy policies.

The application does not intentionally integrate advertising services or advertising SDKs.

## 6. Advertising

DCVG Data Logger does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services for targeted advertising.

## 7. Data Sharing

The application does not provide a user account system or a developer-operated service for sharing survey records.

Survey reports may be shared by users through Android's sharing functionality.

Information handled by the Android operating system or third-party services is subject to the privacy practices of those providers.

## 8. Data Security

The application relies on Android's application sandbox and the security mechanisms provided by the operating system to protect locally stored information.

Users are responsible for securing their devices and protecting exported reports and files.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Survey records remain on the device until they are deleted by the user or removed through application data management.

Users can delete application data through the Android system settings or uninstall the application.

Because Android backup functionality may be enabled, some eligible data may remain in an Android backup according to the user's backup settings and applicable retention rules.

Users are encouraged to export important survey reports before clearing application data or uninstalling the application.

## 10. Children's Privacy

DCVG Data Logger is designed for professional industrial inspection activities and is intended for adult users.

The application is not designed for children.
